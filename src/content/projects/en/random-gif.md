## Context

So I don't have to hunt for the right GIF to express myself in a conversation. I built a bot to do it for me.

## How it works

A `/random-gif` command in Discord. With no argument, the bot picks at random. With one or more keywords, it picks from matching results.

## Today

The project is a few years old. I redeployed it on the cluster in 2026 and moved the server layer over to Hono. Live, used on Discord servers with my friends.
